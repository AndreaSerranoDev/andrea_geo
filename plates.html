BENEATH YOUR FEET

Continents aren’t plates.

Tectonic plates are pieces of the lithosphere: Earth’s crust and the rigid uppermost mantle. A plate can carry both a continent and ocean floor. The pale land outlines help you find your bearings.

ABOUT THIS MAP

A simplified learning model.

This game includes 12 selected plates. Other plates remain visible but aren’t part of the quiz. Boundaries follow the PB2002 model; plate definitions can differ across models. Sizes are distorted near the poles.
